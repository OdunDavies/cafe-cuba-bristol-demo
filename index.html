A small Cuban kitchen, open to the street.

Cafe Cuba cooks Cuban and Caribbean plates a few metres from the table — ropa vieja, roast pork, plantain, yuca, and mojitos — in a room that regulars treat as a neighbourhood table rather than a chain.

Open kitchen

Cooked to order, beside the tables.

The Bristol Sauce described a glass-fronted room next to Turbo Island, with about a dozen tables facing a small steel-lined kitchen.

Tripadvisor reviewers note seating for about 20 covers, plus a couple of outside tables in good weather. Evenings fill — call ahead.

The room

Home cooking, not a tourist set.

The Bristol Sauce (February 2025) reported that Cafe Cuba and Morales Cafe are part of a small family set of businesses linked to Osvaldo Morales, described as a former coffee farmer from Guantánamo province, and David Otero, described as a former barman at the Hotel Guantánamo. The same piece said Cafe Cuba opened on Stokes Croft after Osvaldo arrived in Bristol, and that the kitchen plays cubaton, bachata and dembow at a tolerable volume.

Morales Cafe, at 192 Cheltenham Road, is a related daytime cafe according to that article — daughters Omara and Ana were named as running it. Treat that as press reporting, not a claim this demo can verify independently.

What guests mention

Generous plates, strong cocktails, a short room.

Bristol Live called it a hidden gem on Stokes Croft and singled out ropa vieja and the mojitos. Tripadvisor guests mention vegan choices, flan, and staff finding a high chair without fuss. Several say the room is small and booking is the sensible move at night.

From published menus and reviews

Plates people come back for.

Prices move. Bristol Live (2022) listed ropa vieja at £14.99. The Bristol Sauce (2025) listed ropa vieja, roast pork, and a rice and prawn dish at £18.99, with most cocktails at £8.50. Confirm on the night.

House plate

Ropa vieja

Shredded beef stewed with garlic, onion, peppers, olives and tomato, served with rice and black beans, avocado and fried plantain — as described by Bristol Live and The Bristol Sauce. A vegan ropa vieja has also been listed.

From the pan

Roast pork & arroz con pollo

Marinated roast pork is a Cuban favourite on the menu in press write-ups. Arroz con pollo has been praised on Tripadvisor for generous portions, cooked from scratch.

To start or share

Tamales, tostones, yuca

Maize tamales, fried plantain (tostones / chicharita), and yuca — boiled with garlic sauce or fried — show up across menu scrapes and the Bristol Sauce review.

From the sea

Prawns & seafood casserole

A tomato and white-wine prawn rice and a seafood casserole have been listed publicly. HappyCow notes a separate vegan section including ropa vieja and tostones.

After

Flan

Flan is a repeated Tripadvisor mention, including a “flan de tres leches” line on a third-party menu scrape. Cuban coffee is another closer guests write about.

Glass

Mojito & daiquiri

Guests repeatedly call the mojitos a reason to stay. The Bristol Sauce described a daiquiri shaken rather than packed with ice, and a mojito with an extra band of rum.

How to get a table

Call. The room does not hold a walk-in rush.

  1. Phone 0117 329 4722 and say how many, and whether you need a high chair.
  2. Lunch is the quieter sitting, according to guests. Evenings and weekends are when people advise booking.
  3. Tell them about allergies when you book. A vegan list has been available; it is still a meat-forward kitchen.

Hours as listed on Tripadvisor

Tue–Sat 12:00–22:00 · Sun 17:00–22:00 · Mon closed.

Other directories sometimes show Friday and Saturday until 23:00. Check before you travel.

Stokes Croft

69 Stokes Croft, BS1 3QP

A short walk from the city centre, on the stretch locals know for independent rooms rather than hotel restaurants. Caribbean Croft is a few doors away at 30 Stokes Croft with its own site and a Jamaican menu — a different kitchen, useful if this room is full.

Takeaway and delivery are listed on Tripadvisor. Card payments are accepted. Outdoor seating is listed when the weather allows.

Before you go

Questions guests usually ask.

Do I need to book?

Evenings, yes if you can. Guests and local press both say the room is small and fills. Lunch is described as quieter.

Is there a vegan menu?

HappyCow and Tripadvisor guests describe a vegan or vegetarian list, including a vegan ropa vieja and potaje de frijoles. It is still a meat-led kitchen.

Can I bring a buggy?

One December 2025 Tripadvisor review says staff found a high chair for a 10-month-old. The room is small, so call first.

Is this the official website?

No. Public listings point to a Facebook page, not an owned site. This page is a concept demo and is not affiliated with Cafe Cuba.
